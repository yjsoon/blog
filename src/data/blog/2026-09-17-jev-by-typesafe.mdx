This is a very, very interesting new AI model that just dropped today - Jev by [typesafe.ai](https://typesafe.ai/)

What to know:

- It’s not a language model. It reads text and, based on your inputs, drops a decision for you. It doesn’t generate text.
- It’s very fast and very cheap: $0.042 per million input tokens and $0 output (well there isnt much output)
- Folks online have used it for extremely speedy and cheap “computer use” use cases - getting the model to decide what to do next, and it’s drastically outperformed all the existing LLMs.
- Yeah it’s “just” a classifier. Classifiers aren’t new, but classifiers based on text this fast and cheap are impressive!
- The onboarding is super cool! Quizzes you and gives a playground to really drive home the point this ain’t an LLM

If you’re unsure what to build with it, send its docs to your favourite AI that’s connected to your GitHub account and ask it to look at your 20 most recently active repos to provide suggestions on where it might fit!
